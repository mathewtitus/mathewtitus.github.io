Biography

Mathew completed a postdoc in complex systems analysis in 2020,

after receiving his doctorate in mathematics under the supervision of Ed Waymire. During that time he began consulting in the water sector, as a local harmful algal bloom (HAB) had just threatened nearby Salem, Oregon's drinking water supply. Since then, he has worked with them to create an early warning system for HABs, as well as performed research for DARPA, The Water Research Foundation, and maintained an active connection to academia, giving talks and publishing articles in data science and mathematics.

He is currently developing an online tool to enable researchers

to easily put the Stan statistical modeling toolbox to use. This has been used to study salmon survival rates in the Pacific Northwest; see this study. In software engineering, he is developing a custom transformer-based model for generating graphic novel illustrations. Other interests include learning the standard model of particle physics, and characterizing fractals in various function spaces.
