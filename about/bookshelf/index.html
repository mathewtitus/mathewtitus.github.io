Bookshelf

By Mathew Titus

February 25, 2024

A short list of books that either left an impression or that I have been reading / have read recently.

Math

Functional Analysis

  • M. Reed & B. Simon, Methods of Modern Mathematical Physics (Vols. I and II)

Analysis

  • F. Jones, Lebesgue Integration on Euclidean Space
  • N. L. Carothers, Real Analysis
  • L.V. Ahlfors, Complex Analysis
  • T. Needham, Visual Complex Analysis

Probability

  • G. Grimmett & D.R. Stirzaker, Probability and Random Processes
  • P. Mörters & Y. Peres, Brownian Motion
  • R. Bhattacharya & E.C. Waymire, A Basic Course in Probability Theory
  • R. Bhattacharya & E.C. Waymire, Continuous Parameter Markov Processes and Stochastic Differential Equations
  • H. McKean, Stochastic Integrals
  • D.W. Stroock & S.R.S. Varadhan, Multidimensional Diffusion Processes
  • T. Tao, Topics in Random Matrix Theory
  • J. Pearl, Causality

Geometry

  • J.M. Lee, Introduction to Smooth Manifolds
  • M.P. doCarmo, Riemannian Geometry

Algebra

I know very little algebra, but anywhere Analysis and Geometry are found Algebra should get an invite. In undergraduate I studied from T. Hungerford’s Abstract Algebra, which I remember enjoying.

Physics

  • M. Peskin & D. Schroeder, Introduction to Quantum Field Theory
  • C. Rovelli, The Order of Time
  • C.W. Misner, K.S. Thorne, & J.A. Wheeler, Gravitation
  • D. Griffiths, Introduction to Quantum Mechanics
  • D. Griffiths, Introduction to Electrodynamics
  • T. Frankel, The Geometry of Physics

Literature

  • J. Williams, Stoner
  • D. DeLillo, The Names
  • D.F. Wallace, Infinite Jest
  • T. Pynchon, Crying of Lot 49
  • P.K. Dick, A Scanner Darkly
  • R.M. Pirsig, Zen and the Art of Motorcycle Maintenance
  • C. Potok, The Chosen

Foreign language

  • J.K. Rowling, Harry Potter e la Camera dei Segreti
  • P. Coelho, L’Alchimista
  • H. Hesse, Siddhartha

Poetry

  • Hafiz, The Gift
  • Anything by Rumi

For fun

  • C. McDougall, Born to Run
  • H. Murakami, What I Talk About When I Talk About Running
  • B. Heinrich, Why We Run: A Natural History
  • A. Lamott, Bird by Bird
  • J.K. Lopez-Alt, The Food Lab
  • S. El-Waylly, Start Here
  • M. Beard, SPQR
  • A. Weir, Hail Mary
  • D. Hofstadter, Gödel, Escher, Bach: an Eternal Golden Braid
